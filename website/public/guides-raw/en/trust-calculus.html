Trust Calculus in 3D

In this lesson's teaching model, each piece of evidence is a vector along three independent axes: F (Formality), G (Granularity), R (Reliability). To pick a hypothesis after the ADI analysis we do not average these axes. Trust is capped by the weakest link - trust = min(F, G, R). This illustrates WLNK here, not a universal FPF formula. Every number on this page is a teaching set: the 0-9 axes and the F+G+R=12 threshold were invented to show the principle, not taken from a real measurement. How Forgeplan actually rates evidence - at the end of the page.

The three axes every claim is rated along

The 0-9 scale on each axis is this lesson's scale; the axes are independent. One piece of evidence may be strict but come from an unreliable source - the weakest link decides everything.

Seven pieces of evidence · what the Trust Calculus shows

The WLNK column = min(F, G, R). It is neither a sum nor an average - it is the weakest link of the vector. Compare that with how the "average score" deceives when one axis collapses.

The average deceives · WLNK versus Average

Take E5 (a vendor whitepaper): F=7, G=8, R=3. The average = 6 - it looks solid. But R=3 means it is marketing material: the source is selling the product. For a production decision a weak R is not compensated by a nice wording. WLNK = 3 says it honestly: you cannot use it as the basis of a decision.

What Forgeplan actually does

Forgeplan computes an artifact's F/G/R on a 0–1 scale and their geometric mean (F × G × R)^(1/3). These are artifact-quality heuristics, not this lesson's 0–9 evidence ratings; the product has no sum threshold. An evidence pack's intrinsic score (kind: evidence) does not use these teaching F/G/R values. In the full scorer, an unexpired pack scores max(0, verdict − CL penalty − type modifier). Bases for supports / weakens / refutes are 1.0 / 0.5 / 0.0; CL0 / CL1 / CL2 / CL3 penalties are 0.9 / 0.4 / 0.1 / 0; modifiers for measurement / test / benchmark / audit are 0 / 0 / 0.1 / 0.2. An optional valid_until, if expired, takes precedence: the score is 0.1, including for refutes; with no date, this scorer applies no expiry. Recursive decision R_eff takes the minimum of its own eligible evidence score and eligible dependency contributions. Evidence packs in draft count; superseded and deprecated packs are excluded. Dependencies also exclude draft and the note/memory kinds. A dependency contributes max(0, dependency R_eff − relation penalty): based_on/informs subtract 0.1 (CL2), refines/depends_on subtract 0 (CL3). For example, own evidence at 1.0 and an eligible based_on dependency at 0.2 produce 0.1 overall. With no eligible own evidence, an ordinary decision scores 0; a leaf evidence pack is scored on its own fields. The flat helper used for some displayed scores neither traverses dependencies nor applies the type modifier; it is not interchangeable with the full scorer. The verdicts on this page use the same keys, but the F/G/R ratings and E1…E7 set are a teaching example.
