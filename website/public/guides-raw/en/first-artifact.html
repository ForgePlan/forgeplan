The ten authored steps show routing → shape → validate → reason → build → prove → activate. No project was changed, no tests ran, and no current R_eff or health result was measured. In a real Standard task, complete the RFC and required checks as well.

Practices to take away:

  • Route first. Assess the task’s depth before choosing its artifacts.
  • Shape before code. Record why the change is needed and what it must do.
  • Validate early. Inspect actual findings; a template check is not behavioral proof.
  • Reason with ADI. Compare alternatives and test their consequences.
  • Prove with evidence. Link inspectable observations. R_eff is an implementation-specific heuristic, not a correctness probability.
  • Activate when ready. Satisfy the applicable gates. Active files remain editable; record substantial changes with explicit lineage.
